Notifications

Browser alerts bring you back to a session that needs a look.

  1. Allow this site to notify you.

  2. Allow alerts on your computer.

    On Mac: System Settings → Notifications → your browser or this website → Allow notifications. Make sure Focus isn’t silencing it.

    Notification settings guide ↗
  3. Keep this dashboard open.

    Another tab is fine. Keep the browser running and your computer awake.

  4. Check that a test reaches you.

When we notify you

We alert when recorded cost reaches at least 3× its matched local median and the next 10 prompts are forecast above $4.

Repeat alerts wait at least 10 minutes and require both spending and excess cost to rise.
